01

The brief

Years buying media for 200+ eCommerce brands — $100M+ in spend — and the same failure on repeat: the competitor's move always surfaced weeks after it started working.

The problem

A rival launches a new funnel and cuts prices on Monday. Your ROAS slips by Thursday. Nobody connects the two until someone opens their ad library — three weeks and a lot of ad spend later. Competitor watching was open tabs, screenshots and luck.

The build

An analyst that never sleeps: capture every channel daily, diff it against yesterday, decode what changed and why it's working, then write the counter-move for your catalogue and price points. Grounded in captured evidence — never a model's guess.

Status

Live, in production, watching real brands daily. Private beta with founding customers onboarding in batches. Three public demo dossiers run on real household-name brands so anyone can inspect the output before asking for access.
