Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

WatchBack ("we", "us") operates watchback.ai, a competitor-intelligence service for eCommerce brands. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. Questions: marin.bando@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Your brand & watchlist — your store URL, product/pricing information you provide or that we read from your public store, and the competitors you ask us to monitor.
  • Billing data — handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number; we keep only your Stripe customer reference and subscription status.
  • Usage data — standard logs (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) to keep the service secure and improve it.
  • Advertising data — we use the Meta Pixel and Meta's Conversions API on our marketing pages to measure ads (see Cookies below).

Competitor data

The intelligence we produce is compiled exclusively from publicly available materials: public ad libraries, public social profiles, public storefronts, and marketing emails sent to their subscriber lists. We do not access private accounts or non-public systems.

How we use your data

  • To provide the service: building your dossiers, sending your daily briefs (including to a Slack webhook you configure).
  • To bill you via Stripe.
  • To communicate with you about your account.
  • To measure and improve our marketing (Meta Pixel / Conversions API).

We do not sell your personal data. Your watchlist and dossiers are private to your account.

Processors we use

Railway (hosting), Vercel (website), Stripe (payments), Meta (advertising measurement), Slack (only if you connect it), and email/scraping infrastructure providers acting on our instructions.

Cookies & tracking

Our marketing pages use the Meta Pixel, which sets cookies to measure advertising. You can block cookies in your browser; the site keeps working. We also send conversion events server-side via Meta's Conversions API (hashed identifiers only).

Retention & deletion

We keep account data while your account is active. Email marin.bando@gmail.com to delete your account and personal data; we'll complete deletion within 30 days, except records we must keep for tax/accounting.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. Email us and we'll honour them.

Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will be announced by email.
