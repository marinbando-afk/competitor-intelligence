Fair questions, straight answers

How is this different from checking the Meta Ad Library myself?

The Ad Library shows you a wall of ads for one brand, once, with no memory. WatchBack captures every competitor every day, remembers everything, and tells you what changed and what it means — including the whitelisted creator and persona pages that never appear under the brand's own name.

How do I know the insights are accurate?

Every claim in a brief links to its evidence — the ad, the page, the screenshot. When something is an interpretation rather than a fact, it says so ("seems like"). Credibility is the product; we'd rather tell you less than tell you wrong.

Is this AI-generated fluff?

The data is captured, not generated: real ads, real pages, real screenshots, stored daily. We use AI for one job — reading what was captured and explaining it the way a sharp analyst would. Every conclusion stays pinned to the underlying evidence you can click.

What do I need to set up?

Nothing on your side. No pixel, no integration, no access to your ad account. Type your competitors' websites, confirm their social pages, connect Slack — done in about 3 minutes.

What if my competitors barely advertise?

Then you'll know that too — quiet is a signal. But most founders discover the opposite: satellite funnels, whitelisted pages and email flows they had no idea existed.
