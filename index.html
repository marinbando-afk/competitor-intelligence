The debrief

Questions sharp operators ask

Isn't this just generic AI making things up?

No. It reads only what we captured from that competitor — their actual ads, posts, emails and storefront — and every insight links to the exact source. If it can't point to it, it doesn't say it. Don't take it on faith: open the live dossiers and judge yourself.

Is this legal? Will they know I'm watching?

Everything comes from public materials — public ad libraries, public profiles, their public storefront, newsletters they send subscribers. Ordinary competitive research, done systematically. And no — there's nothing to detect on their side. The uncomfortable part: the sharpest brands already run this playbook on you.

What exactly gets monitored?

Four channels per competitor, daily: paid ads (every live ad, scripts transcribed, hooks named), organic social (IG, TikTok, Facebook with real engagement), email (their actual campaigns and codes), and storefront (prices to the cent, with dated before/after screenshots).

What do I have to set up?

Almost nothing — no install, no pixel, no ad-account access. Name your competitors, confirm the matched social pages, one 15-second newsletter signup per competitor. First dossier within 24 hours.

How are the recommendations specific to MY brand?

At onboarding we scan your store too. Every insight comes with a play that names your actual products and prices — not fortune-cookie advice.
